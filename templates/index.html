    System Design

    Engineering Architecture

    Architected with separation of concerns, containerized pipelines, and zero persistent PI.

    Model Serialization & Preprocessing

    Calibrated scikit-learn models serialized via Joblib. Implements median imputation for missing values, frequency encoding, and log-transform scaling on income features.

    Safety, Validation & Privacy

    Strict schema enforcement via Marshmallow. Zero Personally Identifiable Information (PII) required or saved. Parameterized SQL queries prevent injection.

    Serving & Optimization

    Flask 3.x web application served by Gunicorn on Render. Features in-memory caching, gzip compression, rate limiting with IP bucket quotas, and health check endpoints.
